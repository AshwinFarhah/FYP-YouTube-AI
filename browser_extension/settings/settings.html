About NeuroShield AI

NeuroShield AI is an explainable, AI-powered YouTube content assessment system designed to support parental awareness of children's digital content exposure.

Assessment Approach Hybrid AI Framework
Machine Learning Random Forest
Content Analysis Multimodal Features
Explainability SHAP
Developmental Assessment Cognitive, Behavioural & Emotional

NeuroShield AI provides Hybrid Developmental analysis content assessments to support parental decision-making. Results are estimates based on extracted video features and do not constitute a clinical or psychological diagnosis.
